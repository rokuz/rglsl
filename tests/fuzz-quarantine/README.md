Shaders of `cargo xtask fuzz --random` whose glslang module hangs, resets or crashes the driver of an exec target, confirmed alone, and family references whose image on a target differs from the image the other targets agree on (`reference-differs`): `<target>/<mode>/<family>-<shader>-<hash>/` holds the shader, its `.json` shader job and `reason.toml` (verdict, messages of both runs, exit, seed, generator hash, date, driver); `<mode>` is `O0` (glslang) or `O` (glslang -O), and rglsl does not run a shader with the same text and job on that target in that mode.
